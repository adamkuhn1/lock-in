## Steps
1. **Read everything** and work out the numbers with a Python script in {{work_dir}} (`python3`, standard library only). Check your totals against the raw rows. 2. **Write the report** as one self-contained HTML file, `{{work_dir}}/report.html`. Draw every chart as inline SVG generated by your script: no CDNs, no JavaScript, no web fonts, so it prints exactly as written. Use US Letter pages (`@page { size: letter; margin: 0.6in }`), a restrained palette with one accent color, a clear type scale and `break-inside: avoid` on every chart and table. Sections, in order:
   - **Cover**: his name, school, degree and graduation, "Recruiting season report", the date range, and his links (GitHub, LinkedIn, portfolio, LeetCode) as clickable links with the full URL visible.
   - **At a glance**: 6 to 8 big numbers (days in, active days, longest streak, problems solved, applications, interviews, offers, emails sent) and a three-sentence summary of how the season is going.
   - **Activity**: a GitHub-style heatmap of every season day, plus a weekly bar chart split by type of work (LeetCode, applications, emails, commits, prep).
   - **LeetCode**: solved by difficulty, solved by topic, the topic roadmap with his level for each topic, solves per week, and how his ratings (nailed / struggled / help) changed over time. List the hardest problems he has solved.
   - **Applications**: the funnel (applied → OA → interview → offer) with conversion rates, applications per week against his weekly target, the stage breakdown, the companies he applied to most, and a table of every application (company, role, term, location, applied date, stage). Use small type for the table, and let it run onto more pages if it needs to.
   - **Outreach**: emails sent per week against target, reply rate, follow-ups, and the contacts he reached (name, title, company). Don't print guessed email addresses.
   - **Projects**: one block per project: what it is (from its README if the folder is on this Mac, otherwise from the resume and commits), its GitHub link, commits over time, and its idea list (done, in progress, to do).
   - **Interview prep**: stories by tag and when each was last practiced, the fundamentals checklist progress, company prep and upcoming interviews from the calendar.
   - **Resume**: a one-page summary of his experience, projects and skills from the base resume, plus a list of every tailored resume (company, role, file name).
   - **What's next**: 4 to 6 specific, honest recommendations drawn from the data (for example "3 of 25 OAs became interviews: two timed mock OAs a week"). No generic advice.
   Leave out a section or a chart, and say so in one line, when there's no data for it. Never make up numbers.

   mkdir -p "{{out_dir}}"
