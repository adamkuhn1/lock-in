## Problem bank
420 problems: all of NeetCode 250 (which contains NeetCode 150) plus 170 supplements.
- 413 are free and 7 are premium. Premium ones are flagged `paidOnly` and carry a free neetcode.io link.
- Supplements come from LeetCode topic tags, ranked by how many lists include them (Blind 75, Grind 169, LeetCode 75, Top Interview 150) and by like ratio.
- Free problems: 103 easy, 242 medium, 68 hard.
- Advanced Graphs and 2-D DP have no real easy problems. LeetCode has no "intervals" tag, so the Intervals topic relies on NeetCode labels.

## Evidence behind the mastery model
- The goal is recognizing the pattern in problems you haven't seen, within time: mediums in about 20–25 minutes ([NeetCode](https://neetcode.io/courses/lessons/how-to-use-neetcode-effectively)). Learn patterns, don't memorize answers ([Tech Interview Handbook](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-prep/)).
- Grind's time targets: easy 15–20 min, medium 20–35, hard 30–45 ([Grind 75](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75/)).
- Look at the solution after 15–20 minutes with no progress, then re-implement it yourself ([USACO Guide](https://usaco.guide/general/practicing)).
- Re-solve on a spaced schedule; one practitioner schedule is 1, 2–4, 8–16, 32–64 days ([Red-Green-Code](https://www.redgreencode.com/leetcode-tip-13-spaced-repetition-interval-lengths/)). The best review gap shrinks relative to how far away the test is ([Cepeda et al. 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19076480/)).
- Mixing topics: 72% vs 38% on a delayed test in a classroom trial, because students learn to choose the strategy ([Rohrer et al. 2014](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13423-014-0588-3)).
- Solve count correlates weakly with interview performance (r = 0.27) and levels off around 500 problems. Hards help more once you have about 100 mediums ([interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io/blog/how-well-do-leetcode-ratings-predict-interview-performance)).
- Weak evidence: no study covers spaced repetition of algorithm problems specifically, and the topic-level thresholds are design choices.
